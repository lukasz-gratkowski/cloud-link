Downloads shared folders and single files from OneDrive and Google Drive share links
Recreates a shared folder on disk with all its subfolders
Paste one link or several, or drop a link on the window; a link on the clipboard is picked up automatically
Continues interrupted files from where they stopped, after a lost connection, Stop, a restart or a reboot
Checks every file against the size and checksum the service reports, and downloads a mismatch again
Retries network errors, stalls and throttling by itself, and waits when the network is gone
One progress bar for the whole job with speed and time left, and a status for every file
Filters that show only the files in progress or only the files with problems
Exports Google Docs, Sheets and Slides as .docx, .xlsx and .pptx files
Sign-in happens in your browser; CloudLink never sees a password
Download only: never uploads, changes or deletes files in the cloud
Downloaded files are tagged as coming from the internet, so Windows and Office apply their usual caution
Light and dark theme, following Windows
No telemetry and no advertising; free and open source under the MIT licence
